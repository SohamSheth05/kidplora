Kidplora privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Kidplora is a children's learning app developed by Soham Robinkumar sheth. Children do not need an account. Learning progress is stored on the device. Free play includes Google AdMob advertising, which involves data processing by Google as explained below.

Information stored on your device

Kidplora stores language and sound settings, age/learning tier, an optional first name, game difficulty and progress, session counts, stickers and coins, saved outfits and trains, garden progress, and legacy ad-free access status. Kidplora does not send the optional first name or saved learning progress to a developer-operated server. We do not operate a child-account service, cloud progress system, or developer analytics service.

Advertising and Google's services

Kidplora is free and uses Google AdMob. Ads are placed on the home screen and at paced transitions between sessions, rather than inside gameplay or the Parent Zone. All activities, learning packs, sticker pages and plants are included without purchases or subscriptions. Previously saved ad-free access, if present, is respected.

AdMob may process IP addresses, app and device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. IP addresses can indicate an approximate location. Kidplora configures ad requests for child-directed treatment and a G maximum content rating. It does not request personalized advertising and removes the Android advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions.

These settings do not mean that AdMob receives no data. Google's processing is described in Google's Privacy Policy and its Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. We do not add other advertising or analytics SDKs.

Advertising privacy choices

Kidplora uses Google's User Messaging Platform to refresh advertising privacy status when the app opens. All players are treated as under the age of consent. Ads are requested only when Google's SDK reports they may be requested; learning activities remain available when ads cannot load or privacy status is unavailable. If Google requires privacy choices, parents can review them in the gated Parent Zone. Under-age treatment is also set separately on ad requests.

Speech, microphone and location

Spoken prompts use installed offline Android text-to-speech voices. Kidplora does not record microphone audio, collect precise location, or request access to your contacts, camera, or photos. Missing speech voices may need to be installed separately through Android settings.

Children and parental choices

Kidplora is designed for children with parent or caregiver involvement. Children are not asked to create accounts or provide contact details. A first name is optional and stored locally. Parents can adjust language, learning tier, sound and daily session-count limits in the gated Parent Zone.

Retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on the device until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Kidplora disables Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup for its app data. Google manages retention of information it processes under its own policies.

This developer website

This site contains information and support links. It has no advertising, analytics scripts, sign-in, or contact form. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and request details, to deliver the site and maintain security. An email you choose to send to support includes the information you put in that email. Please avoid sending a child's personal information when requesting support.

Contact and policy updates

For support or privacy questions, parents and caregivers can email srsheth20@gmail.com. Developer: Soham Robinkumar sheth.

We may update this policy as the app or its services change. The current version and update date will be available on this page.
